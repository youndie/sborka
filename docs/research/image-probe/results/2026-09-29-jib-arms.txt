## digests across arms
arm 1b and arm 2 differ because their binaries differ: editing build.gradle.kts makes the tree
dirty, kore-build then takes a new builtAt (B-28), and the relink is a different binary
(5eba7a26… vs ed94d0ff…). Not a property of the layers.
